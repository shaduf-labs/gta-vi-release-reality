Pool topic: GTA VI Release Reality

Question revision: 1

Exact question: What is the current release reality of GTA VI: what has Rockstar officially committed to, what has materially changed, and how should credible new evidence affect confidence in the expected release?

Research report · October 29, 2026

GTA 6 review codes are reportedly moving; the date has not changed

Rockstar, Take-Two, PlayStation, and Xbox still list November 19, 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. New reporting says review codes have begun going to media, with reviews expected November 18. That is a limited, unofficial launch-operations signal, not proof that the game is finished or that the date cannot move.

Official commitment: November 19 remains listed

Rockstar’s GTA VI page and the checked Xbox and PlayStation pages list November 19, 2026 for the console launch. Take-Two’s latest results release also names that date. No new official date change appeared in the pages checked. The date remains a public commitment, not a delivery guarantee. Rockstar has moved its public target twice before: from Fall 2025 to May 26, 2026, then to November 19, 2026.

Reported signal: review access is not a gold announcement

On October 26, the NateTheHate2 account posted that Rockstar had begun distributing review codes to media and that the embargo was November 18. Insider Gaming reported the same review date and said it could independently verify reports of the date. The article says review copies allegedly went out near the end of October. It does not name an outlet that received a code or explain how it independently checked the date. On October 14, Insider Gaming had reported that reviews would appear before launch but said its preliminary embargo date could change.

What this supports: If accurate, review-code distribution and a pre-launch embargo show that a review process is being scheduled close to launch. This modestly supports active launch operations.

What it does not support: No checked Rockstar or Take-Two source announced the code schedule. No named recipient was independently identified in the reporting inspected. Review access alone does not establish that the game has gone gold, is final or fully certified, or cannot be delayed. Two earlier official target movements still limit certainty. The report changes the confidence picture slightly; it does not change the official date.

Some commenters in one self-selected r/GTAVI thread asked whether review access means the game has “gone gold” and raised spoiler concerns. Those comments show a possible reader inference, not how common it is. No reviewer receipt was verified in that thread.

Other release questions remain open

No official native PC version, date, or window has been announced. GamesBeat still reports a Q1 2027 PC plan from four anonymous sources; it is not an official commitment. The checked US, Spain, and New Zealand PlayStation Store pages list different regional PS5 unlock times; the checked Xbox page lists November 19 but no exact hour. Rockstar Support says preload begins November 12 at local midnight; preload is not the playable unlock.

The last detailed report on the Cyberleek claim found that a return notice was reported, but no new material had appeared on the public list by October 22. The account, operator, and full-build claim remain unverified. The report does not change the release date or confidence; see the October 25 evidence review for the source history, community context, missing evidence, and verdict. No newer independently verified material surfaced in this bounded search.

Next official checkpoint

Take-Two has scheduled its fiscal Q2 2027 results for November 9, 2026. The event notice confirms the date, not what the company will say about GTA VI. Recheck Rockstar and the platform pages for a commitment change; do not infer one from a review schedule alone. No numeric release probability is supported.

Sources: Rockstar · Take-Two fiscal Q1 results · PlayStation · Xbox · NateTheHate2 post · Insider Gaming, October 26 · Insider Gaming, October 14
