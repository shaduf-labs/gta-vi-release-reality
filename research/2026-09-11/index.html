Status — September 11, 2026

Is GTA 6 still on for November 19?

Yes, on the current public record. Rockstar, Take-Two, PlayStation, and Xbox still list November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. PlayStation’s store pages now expose regional PS5 unlock timestamps. That makes the launch plan more concrete, not the date guaranteed. No official PC platform or PC release date is published in the sources checked.

Checked September 11, 2026 · 69 calendar days to the listed console date · Question revision 1

What changed since the last check?

PlayStation Store pages now show regional digital-PS5 unlock times: 11:00 UTC on November 18 for New Zealand and 05:00 UTC on November 19 for the United States, with other regions in between. These are operational Store timestamps, not a new global date and not a guarantee that the Store cannot change.

PlayStation also tied two limited-edition DualSense controllers to the November 19 launch, with pre-orders opening September 10. That is another coordinated launch-campaign signal. It does not prove the build is finished.

Verdict: no new official delay was found in the current Rockstar Newswire, Take-Two, PlayStation, Xbox, support, and Store pages inspected today.

PS5 launch times: useful, but still a Store observation

New official Store detail: the US PlayStation Store lists the digital PS5 version for 05:00 UTC on November 19. The New Zealand Store lists it for 11:00 UTC on November 18. The local calendar date remains November 19 in each market.

US Store page · New Zealand Store page

Limit: GTAVice counted 15 regional windows across 63 countries from Store pages, and GamesRadar and Windows Central republished the finding. That is useful secondary analysis, but this report does not turn it into an Xbox schedule. Account region, product terms, and Store timestamps can change before launch.

For a buyer, the safe move is simple: check the Store for the country tied to your account again before launch. Do not treat a countdown or a region-switch tip as an official promise.

PC rumor check

Unofficial claim; not an official commitment. Source and evidence are summarized below. Spoiler status: none. Uncertainty status: high.

Claim: a September 4 report relayed VGC editor-in-chief Andy Robinson saying there was “a bit of noise” that the PC version might arrive sooner than expected. The report gave no date or window.

New context: PC Gamer’s current release explainer says its writer’s best guess is that a PC version would not arrive before November 2027, based on historical Rockstar port gaps. That is an editorial forecast, not a source-backed window, and it does not cancel the “sooner” rumor.

Verdict: the PC answer is unchanged. Rockstar, Take-Two, PlayStation, and Xbox launch materials checked today list the November 19 console launch, not a PC version. “Sooner” could mean a shorter later gap; it does not mean day-one PC.

What would change this: Rockstar or Take-Two publishing a PC platform listing, release window, or date. Historical gaps and an outlet’s estimate remain context, not a schedule.

Leak and malware check: public claims, limited inference

Unofficial claim; not an official commitment. Source and evidence are summarized below. Spoiler status: broad leak context only; no story details or leaked media are reproduced. Uncertainty status: mixed.

Claim: late-August reports described leaked gameplay footage, while GamesRadar reported a claim that some clips were from 2023. PC Gamer reported a targeted Take-Two subpoena request. Neither establishes a release date.

Security warning: Tom’s Hardware reported that a purported 113GB ISO was described by social-media analysts as padded malware. That claim is not independent forensic confirmation, but it is enough to make the safe action clear: do not download an unreleased build or enter credentials into a site promising one.

Verdict: this changes the leak and safety record, not the release answer. Legal action shows an active infringement investigation; it does not show that the build is ready or delayed. The official extended look and Store listings are stronger evidence for the current launch record.

We do not reproduce or link leaked files, footage, private access material, credentials, or instructions.

Reported legal context — not a schedule update

The IWGB says Rockstar faces a final Glasgow Employment Tribunal hearing from September 10 to October 16 over allegations that 31 workers were unlawfully dismissed and blacklisted. PC Gamer reported the hearing timetable and the tribunal decision allowing the allegations to proceed. Rockstar’s position, reported by IWGB, is that the dismissals concerned messages in a private union Discord channel that it says breached confidentiality agreements.

This matters for the company and its workers. It does not currently change the release answer: the sources checked do not link the case to a delay, production stoppage, or new date. That condition would change if Rockstar, Take-Two, or a court filing tied the proceeding to launch execution.

What a buyer can act on now

  • Console launch: PS5/PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S are the listed platforms for November 19, 2026.
  • PS5 digital timing: Store pages currently show regional unlock timestamps; check the country Store tied to your account again before launch.
  • Preload: Rockstar Support says digital preload starts at local midnight on November 12, 2026.
  • Physical copy: the box contains a download code, not a disc; region and account rules matter.
  • Xbox timing: the official platform and date are listed, but this check did not establish a comparable regional unlock schedule.
  • PC: wait for an official platform and date. Do not buy a supposed PC key or treat a rumor window as a commitment.
