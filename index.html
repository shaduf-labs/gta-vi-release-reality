Release status · checked October 9, 2026

GTA 6 release date: is November 19 still listed?

Yes. Rockstar, Take-Two, PlayStation, and Xbox still list November 19, 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. No new official date change was found. Xbox has ruled out the reported exclusive cloud route to PC; no native PC date has been announced.

Previous changesTwo official movementsFall 2025 → May 26, 2026 → November 19, 2026
PCNo date announcedPC streaming also ruled out by Xbox CSO

What changed since October 5?

No release-date or native-platform change was found. A new report briefly suggested Xbox had exclusive cloud-streaming rights for GTA VI and that PC players might use the service at launch. Xbox chief strategy officer Matthew Ball said the game would not stream exclusively or to PC. The Verge updated its report with a correction. This closes the reported PC-cloud route; it does not announce a native PC version or date. Read the October 9 evidence report.

Still unknown: Xbox has not specified whether any cloud service will carry GTA VI or which non-PC devices would support it. The official correction rules out PC streaming and exclusivity, not every possible service arrangement. Do not treat the original report or articles repeating it as independent confirmation.

Take-Two’s latest checked outlook still names November 19 and lists timely delivery among its assumptions. That is a continuing publisher commitment, not a guarantee. Two earlier official date movements remain relevant context.

For physical orders, Rockstar says the boxed version contains a download code, not a disc. It expects the version to be available for shipping or pickup from November 12 to support preload; that does not guarantee a particular retailer’s delivery date. Check the retailer for your order. The playable date remains November 19. Read Rockstar Support’s format and preload terms.

An October 1 r/GTAVI thread marked the move into the final month. It is one self-selected discussion, not a measure of overall demand or evidence that the date will hold. View the discussion.

Earlier unofficial report; not an official commitment. The October 1 report about a performer NDA reminder still traces to one podcast source chain. It was not rechecked in this run and does not establish a schedule change or guarantee. See the October 1 report for its source and limits.

What would change this answer?

A new Rockstar or Take-Two date statement, a material platform listing change, an official PC platform or date, or Xbox’s published cloud-service and device terms. A countdown, campaign event, retailer placeholder, or broad PC forecast is not a schedule update.

Sources: Rockstar, Take-Two, PlayStation, Xbox, The Verge’s corrected report, Matthew Ball’s public statement.
