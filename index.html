Release status · checked October 1, 2026

GTA 6 release date: is November 19 still listed?

Yes. Rockstar, Take-Two, PlayStation, and Xbox still list November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. No official date change was found. PC has no official platform or date.

A new report says some performers were reminded about NDAs and could disclose their involvement on November 19. The report traces to one podcast and has no public email or named recipient. It does not announce a date change or guarantee delivery.

What changed since September 27?

No official schedule change was found. Rockstar’s Newswire still lists the September 24 merchandise announcement as its latest GTA VI post. The collection is scheduled for November 19, and Rockstar says the game is sold separately. The September 17 album announcement also sets that date for the soundtrack. These are launch plans, not proof that the game is finished.

Take-Two’s recent PC comments describe its platform strategy in general. They do not announce GTA VI for PC. PlayStation’s PS5 Pro Enhanced label still has no published performance target or requirement.

What would change this answer?

A new Rockstar or Take-Two schedule statement, a material change to a platform listing, an official PC platform or date, an Xbox release-time statement, or published PS5 Pro performance details. An unverified email report, PC forecast, countdown, stock listing, or merchandise product is not a schedule update.

Sources: Rockstar, Take-Two, Xbox, PlayStation, Rockstar Newswire.
