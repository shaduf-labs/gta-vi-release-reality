Release status · checked October 29, 2026

GTA 6 release date: is November 19 still listed?

Yes. Rockstar, Take-Two, PlayStation, and Xbox still list November 19, 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Reports say review codes have begun going to media and reviews are expected November 18. This is a limited, unofficial launch-operations signal—not proof the game is finished or the date guaranteed.

What the review-code report means

NateTheHate2 posted on October 26 that review codes had begun going to media. Insider Gaming reported a November 18 review date and said copies allegedly went out near the end of October. The article does not name a recipient or explain its independent check. If accurate, review access supports active launch operations. It does not establish that the game has gone gold, is final or certified, or cannot be delayed. Rockstar has changed its public target twice before.

Read the October 29 report and source limits.

PS5 access times are regional Store listings

Official PlayStation Store pages list different digital PS5 timestamps: US, November 19 at 05:00 UTC; Spain, November 18 at 23:00 UTC; New Zealand, November 18 at 11:00 UTC. These are regional product listings, not a publisher-wide global timing announcement. Xbox lists November 19 but no exact hour. Rockstar Support says preload begins November 12 at local midnight; preload is not playable access.

PC remains unconfirmed

Rockstar and Take-Two have not announced a native PC version, date, or window. GamesBeat reports a Q1 2027 plan based on four anonymous sources. Their identities and independence are unknown; do not treat the report as a commitment.

Cyberleek report remains unverified

The last detailed report says an anonymous Cyberleek account posted a return notice but no new material appeared on its public list by October 22. The account, operator, and full-build claim remain unverified. This does not change the official date. Read the October 25 source and evidence review.

Next official checkpoint

Take-Two scheduled fiscal Q2 2027 results for November 9, 2026. The event may provide another schedule check; the event itself is not release evidence.

Sources: Rockstar · Take-Two · PlayStation · Xbox · NateTheHate2 · Insider Gaming
