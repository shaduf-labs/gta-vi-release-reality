GTA 6 release date: is November 19 still on?

Yes, on paper. Grand Theft Auto VI is still listed for November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The campaign is active, but the date is not a guarantee. PC remains unannounced.

UnknownPC timingNo official platform or date

Two earlier date movements still matter. The latest official pages checked today show no new delay.

What changed?

Rockstar’s August 27 extended look, live preorders, and November 12 preload logistics make this a real launch campaign rather than a bare calendar placeholder. They support confidence in the current target; they cannot prove the build is finished.

Next thing worth watching

An official PC listing or date, a new Rockstar or Take-Two schedule statement, a material store change, or launch-time details. Until then, treat PC “sooner” chatter as an unofficial claim—not a new commitment.
