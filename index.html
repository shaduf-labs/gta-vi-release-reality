The launch desk · checked September 19, 2026

GTA 6 release date: is November 19 still on?

Yes, on the current official record. Grand Theft Auto VI is still listed for November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar’s new album rollout and Take-Two’s shareholder remarks add launch-campaign context, not a guarantee. PC remains unannounced.

Two earlier date movements still matter. The official pages checked today show no new delay. An album, preorder, preload, or hardware campaign can show coordinated activity without making the software schedule unchangeable.

What changed?

Rockstar announced a 34-track GTA VI: The Album for November 19, with six singles available and physical pre-orders. Take-Two reiterated the console launch and described PC as increasingly important in general, but did not announce a GTA VI PC platform or window. Reported PS5 Pro scarcity makes buying under pressure a real consumer risk; it is not evidence that the game requires a Pro or is ready.

Next thing worth watching

An official PC listing or date, a new Rockstar or Take-Two schedule statement, a material Store change, an Xbox unlock statement, or a clear PS5 Pro performance statement. Until then, treat album drops, countdowns, stock pressure, and PC forecasts as context—not new commitments.

Sources: Rockstar, Rockstar Newswire, Take-Two meeting transcript, US PlayStation Store, Xbox.
