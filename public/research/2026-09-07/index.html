Status — September 7, 2026

Is GTA 6 still on for November 19?

Yes, on the current public record. Rockstar, Take-Two, PlayStation, and Xbox still list November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The date is locked in on paper: an official commitment, not a guarantee. No official PC platform or PC release date is published in the sources checked.

Checked September 7, 2026 · 73 calendar days to the listed console date · Question revision 1

What changed since the last check?

The launch campaign is now fully active. Rockstar has live preorders and a November 12 preload plan, while its August 27 extended look was presented as in-game footage captured on PlayStation 5. That combination supports confidence in an active launch push. It does not prove the build is finished or make another delay impossible.

Verdict: no new official delay was found in the current Rockstar, Take-Two, PlayStation, Xbox, and launch-support pages inspected today.

PC rumor check

Unofficial claim; not an official commitment. Source and evidence are summarized below. Spoiler status: none. Uncertainty status: high.

Claim: a September 4 report relayed VGC editor-in-chief Andy Robinson saying there was “a bit of noise” that the PC version might arrive sooner than expected. The report gave no date or window.

Best evidence: VICE’s report and Rockstar Report’s summary both keep the claim separate from the official record.

Verdict: the rumor leaves the PC answer unchanged. Rockstar, Take-Two, PlayStation, and Xbox launch materials checked today list the November 19 console launch, not a PC version. “Sooner” could mean a shorter later gap; it does not mean day-one PC.

What would change this: Rockstar or Take-Two publishing a PC platform listing, release window, or date. Historical Rockstar console-to-PC gaps are context, not a schedule.

Leak check: public claim, limited inference

Unofficial claim; not an official commitment. Source and evidence are summarized below. Spoiler status: broad leak context only; no story details or leaked media are reproduced. Uncertainty status: mixed.

Claim: late-August reports described leaked gameplay footage, while GamesRadar reported a claim that some clips were from 2023. Rockstar’s public response acknowledged the leak and its planned official presentation continued; PC Gamer reported that a court granted a targeted Take-Two subpoena request.

Source history and reaction: the reporting draws on a third-party leaker, alleged leakers, social reposts, and later legal reporting. Community reaction is evidence of attention and spoiler risk, not independent confirmation of a build date or launch schedule.

Verdict: this changes the leak record, not the release answer. The age claim is not independently verified; legal action shows that Take-Two treated the material as an infringement investigation, not that the game is ready or delayed. The official extended look is the stronger source for what Rockstar chose to show now.

We do not reproduce or link leaked files, footage, private access material, credentials, or instructions.

What a buyer can act on now

  • Console launch: PS5/PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S are the listed platforms for November 19, 2026.
  • Preload: Rockstar support says preload starts at local midnight on November 12, 2026.
  • Physical copy: the box contains a download code, not a disc; region and account rules matter.
  • PC: wait for an official platform and date. Do not buy a supposed PC key or treat a rumor window as a commitment.
