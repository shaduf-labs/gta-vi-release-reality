GTA 6 platforms, unlock time and preload

Rockstar lists November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Both unlock at midnight local time in every region. Pre-load starts earlier and differs by platform.

QuestionCurrent answerSource
Console releaseNovember 19, 2026 · PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|SRockstar · Rockstar Support
Playable release hourMidnight local time on November 19 in every region on both consoles. This is a rolling local launch, not one simultaneous worldwide UTC moment.Rockstar regional release-time FAQ
PS5 digital pre-loadStarts at local midnight November 10, 2026. Pre-load is not play access.Rockstar Pre-Load FAQ · PlayStation support (Colombia)
Xbox Series X|S digital pre-loadStarts globally at 00:00 GMT November 10. In some western time zones, that is November 9 locally.Rockstar Pre-Load FAQ
How much space?Rockstar says up to 212 GB available depending on device. PlayStation Colombia’s support page tells PS5 players to have 212 GB free. Rockstar gives no exact per-console size or day-one patch figure.Rockstar installation requirements · PlayStation support
Code-in-box versionA download code in the box, no disc; retailer availability from November 12. This is not an early playable release.Rockstar platform editions FAQ
PC platform or dateNot announced by Rockstar. GamesBeat reported Q1 2027 from four anonymous sources; that is not an official PC commitment.GamesBeat · Rockstar
Should a PC-first player buy a console?The Q1 2027 report does not guarantee a PC version by March. Waiting for an official PC listing keeps the console purchase reversible.GamesBeat
Quick distinction: release means playable at the local unlock time; pre-load is only an early download. November 12 is the expected retailer date for code-in-box copies. The official date is a commitment, not a delivery guarantee.
