GTA 6 platforms and buying: what is actually listed?

The official launch scope is PS5/PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S on November 19, 2026. PlayStation Store pages still show regional digital-PS5 unlock times. PC has no official platform or date in the sources checked today.
